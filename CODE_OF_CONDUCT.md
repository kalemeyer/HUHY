# Community standards

HUHY exists so people can raise problems, ask direct questions, contribute
useful work, and disagree without losing their voice.

Be direct without targeting people. Critique ideas, evidence, code, and process;
do not harass, threaten, dox, discriminate, or expose private or sensitive
information. Do not use rank, position, affiliation, or technical experience to
silence someone. Good-faith questions and small problems are welcome.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates the public-information
boundary or these standards. Safety removal is not a project disposition. A
viable proposal may be declined only through the accountable process in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Report ordinary conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Use
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/kalemeyer/HUHY/security/advisories/new)
only for security concerns. Never place sensitive government information in a
conduct or security report to HUHY.

HUHY maintainers will document material moderation decisions when doing so does
not repeat harmful or sensitive content. Retaliation against a good-faith
reporter is not acceptable.
