# Contributing

Contribute only material you are authorized to share publicly. Do not use
government credentials, restricted systems, internal records, sensitive
screenshots, or nonpublic data to develop or test HUHY work.

Before starting:

- Confirm the issue was created through the HUHY safety-reviewed workflow.
- Check the governing public sources and their dates.
- State whether the contribution is private-capacity or authorized official-duty work.
- Confirm ownership and licensing for every dependency, asset, and source.
- Keep test fixtures synthetic and non-identifying.

Pull requests should identify the HUHY issue, explain the user-facing change,
list public sources, describe validation, and name any maintenance impact.

Existing third-party tools remain with their owners. Link or contribute upstream
when invited; do not copy or rebrand another project.
