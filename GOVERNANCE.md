# HUHY governance

Every safe proposal receives a visible disposition. Triage may clarify,
research, combine, refer, or defer work. Triage may not quietly reject it.

Only the named HUHY Steward may mark a viable proposal `huhy:declined`. The
decision must record a plain-language reason and a reopening condition. Prefer
`huhy:not-now` or `huhy:referred` when the need remains valid.

GitHub is the source of truth for approved problems, project work, code review,
releases, and maintainer ownership. The HUHY website provides the public front
door and keeps unreviewed intake in a private safety queue.

During the public beta, [Kale Meyer (`@kalemeyer`)](https://github.com/kalemeyer)
is the named HUHY Steward. Future governance may name a small accountable
council without changing the rule that ordinary triage cannot erase a
submitter&apos;s voice.

The Steward must publish a reason and reopening condition for `huhy:declined`,
recuse from decisions involving a personal conflict, and prefer
`huhy:not-now` or `huhy:referred` when the need remains valid.

Repository administration, security response, and deployment access do not
grant authority to represent the Department of Defense or Department of the Air
Force. HUHY governance applies only to this independent project.
